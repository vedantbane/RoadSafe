About RoadSafe

Building safer roads through citizen participation.

Our Mission

RoadSafe was created to bridge the gap between citizens who face dangerous roads every day and the authorities responsible for fixing them.

Every year, thousands of accidents in India are linked to poor road conditions — potholes, open manholes, debris, and unmarked hazards. Many of these accidents are entirely preventable with timely reporting and repair.

We provide a simple, free platform where anyone can report an issue in under a minute, track its status, and see the collective impact of community action on road safety.

This project is built as a demonstration of how modern web technology can support public good. Data is currently stored locally in your browser.
