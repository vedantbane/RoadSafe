About RoadSafe

Building safer roads through citizen participation.

Our Mission

RoadSafe bridges the gap between citizens who face dangerous roads every day and the authorities responsible for fixing them.

We provide a simple platform where anyone can report an issue, track its status, and see the collective impact of community action on road safety.

This project demonstrates how modern web technology can support public good through timely reporting and transparent progress.
