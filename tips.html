Road Safety Tips

Simple habits that protect you and others on the road.

🕳️

Watch for Potholes

Scan the road 50–100m ahead, especially after rains. Reduce speed on poorly lit roads and avoid sudden swerves.

🌙

Night Driving

Use proper headlights, avoid high beam for oncoming traffic, and increase following distance.

🌧️

Monsoon Caution

Water-filled potholes hide depth. Drive slowly through standing water and never assume the road is intact underneath.

📢

Report Immediately

Report dangerous hazards on RoadSafe as soon as it is safe to do so. Early reports prevent accidents.

↔️

Maintain Distance

Keep a safe following distance. Sudden braking for a pothole can cause rear-end collisions.

🚶

Pedestrian Safety

Use footpaths where available and cross at designated points. Take extra care near broken pavements.

🚗

Vehicle Condition

Regularly check tyre pressure and suspension. Poor roads and underinflated tyres increase blowout risks.

📵

No Distractions

Never use your phone while driving. Your full attention is needed to react safely.
