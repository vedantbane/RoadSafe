Road Safety Tips

Simple habits that protect you and others on the road.

🕳️

Watch for Potholes

Scan the road 50–100m ahead, especially after rains. Reduce speed on poorly lit roads and avoid sudden swerves.

🌙

Night Driving

Use proper headlights, avoid high beam for oncoming traffic, and increase following distance. Many hazards become invisible at night.

🌧️

Monsoon Caution

Water-filled potholes hide depth. Drive slowly through standing water and never assume the road is intact underneath.

📢

Report Immediately

If you spot a dangerous hazard, report it on RoadSafe as soon as it is safe to do so. Early reports prevent accidents.

↔️

Maintain Distance

Keep safe following distance. Sudden braking for a pothole is a major cause of rear-end collisions.

🚶

Pedestrian Safety

Use footpaths where available. Cross at designated points. Be extra careful near broken pavements and open drains.

🚗

Vehicle Condition

Regularly check tyre pressure and suspension. Bad roads + underinflated tyres = higher chance of blowouts.

📵

No Distractions

Never use your phone while driving. On rough roads your full attention is needed to react in time.
